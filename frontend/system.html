What happens to a question

One POST starts it. The events stream over a WebSocket and are kept, so a late connection replays all of them.

EventSent whenWhat the Assistant draws
acceptedThe POST arrived. The server has already answered 202 with a query id.The first node of the route.
intent_detectedThe question was read, by the model or by the rules, and its entities were extracted.The intent, where it was decided, and the entities.
service_callJust before each gRPC call to a service.One node per call, with its milliseconds.
partial_resultA service has answered and more calls may follow.Nothing yet; it is part of the final answer.
order_updateLive tracking only: once per message on the Order service’s gRPC stream.The order stepper moves a step.
final_answer or errorExactly one of them ends every query.The answer, or a failure with Retry.
